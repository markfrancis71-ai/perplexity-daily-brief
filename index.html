Captain Christopher Newport's expedition reached Jamestown, Virginia. The passengers went ashore the next day, founding the first permanent English settlement in the Americas.

The War of the Bavarian Succession ended with the Treaty of Teschen, settling the succession dispute between the Holy Roman Empire and Prussia.

1897
First Wireless Communication Over Open Sea

Guglielmo Marconi sent the world's first wireless communication over open sea, transmitting a message across the Bristol Channel between Lavernock Point and Flat Holm Island.

1913
Sikorsky Flies First Four-Engine Aircraft

Russian-American aviation pioneer Igor Sikorsky flew the world's first four-engine fixed-wing aircraft, the Russky Vityaz, demonstrating that large multi-engine planes were practical.

Fatima Apparitions Begin

Three peasant children near Fatima, Portugal reported seeing a vision of the Virgin Mary. The reported apparitions, returning on the 13th of each month for six months, became one of the most influential events in 20th-century Catholic devotion.

1940

1958
First Use of 'Velcro' Trademark

1981
